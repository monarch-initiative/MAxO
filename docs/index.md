# Documentation for Medical Action Ontology (MAXO)

Medical Action Ontology (MAXO) is a comprehensive open-source computational representation of medical diagnostics, preventions, procedures, interventions, and therapies; MAxO is designed to apply to any patient at any age or stage of life and experiencing any kind of disorder. 
MAXO is closely linked to the [HPO](https://hpo.jax.org/app/){:target="\_blank"}.
